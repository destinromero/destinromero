# The Great Divorce Project — outcomes

- [x] The opening title section clearly features “The Great Divorce” by C.S. Lewis and an editable placeholder for the student’s first and last name.
- [x] The site includes a concise novel overview that establishes the premise, setting, and allegorical nature of the journey without overwhelming the presentation.
- [x] The site includes a visual symbolic-journey section tracing the movement from the grey town toward Heaven and explaining what the major locations represent.
- [x] The site includes an organized character and encounter gallery covering key figures, their conflicts, and what each meeting reveals.
- [x] The themes section explores choice, pride, grace, freedom, attachment, repentance, and transformation with accessible supporting analysis.
- [x] The memorable ideas and quotations section connects brief lines or paraphrased moments to the project’s interpretation.
- [x] The “Choose What to Carry” reflection lets visitors make a symbolic choice and receive a short thematic interpretation.
- [x] Presentation-friendly navigation and section progression make the site easy to move through during a class presentation.
- [x] A polished concluding reflection brings together the journey, themes, and the novel’s central question.
- [x] Layouts remain readable and responsive on classroom screens, laptops, and mobile devices.
